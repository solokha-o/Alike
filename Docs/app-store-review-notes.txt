Thank you for reviewing Alike.

NO ACCOUNT IS NEEDED
Alike has no account, no sign-in and no server, so there is nothing to log
in to; the demo credential fields are intentionally empty. The app opens on
an introduction that hands off to the iOS photo permission alert.

PHOTO LIBRARY ACCESS
Alike compares library photos to find visually similar ones, so photo access
is the core permission. Please grant "Full Access"; with "Limited Access",
select three or four shots of one subject, or the scan reports no groups.

The permission is also used to write. Enhancing a best shot writes a
non-destructive adjustment through PHContentEditingOutput, stamped
com.alike.autoEnhance: iOS keeps the original, no copy is created, and the
edit reverts from either app. It is never automatic — previewed first,
written only on Apply — and not offered for video, in Limited Access, or for
non-editable assets.

Notification permission is requested only for the optional cleanup reminder.

HOME SCREEN WIDGETS
Two widgets, both free, added by long-pressing the home screen and tapping +.
One shows reclaimable space and, after a half-finished review, how far
through it you are with a tap back into it; the other lists similar groups,
screenshots and blurred photos, each row opening that category.

They read a JSON snapshot the app writes into a shared App Group — counts,
byte totals and a date, never image data — and make no network requests. Taps open `alike://` links; two of the library rows are Pro, so
tapping one on a free account reaches that category's paywall, which is
intended.

HOW TO EXERCISE THE APP
1. Step through the introduction and grant photo access in the iOS alert.
2. Tap Scan on the Scanner tab. Analysis runs locally.
3. Open any group. A Best Shot is preselected; use Keep Best Only, Select
   All Except Best, or select by hand. For the enhancement, tap Enhance,
   hold to compare, Apply, then Revert to original.
4. Tap the delete action and confirm. iOS shows its own confirmation.

ON-DEVICE ANALYSIS
All similarity analysis runs on device with the Apple Vision framework. No
photo, thumbnail, feature print or result leaves the device, and the app
makes no network requests except StoreKit's.

BEST SHOT LEARNS FROM YOUR OWN PICKS
Overriding the suggested best shot stores the difference between the two
photos' quality signals — derived numbers, not the photos — to nudge the
ranking towards what you pick. Nothing is collected or sent; the values live
in UserDefaults, Settings resets them, and Delete Alike Data erases them.
App Privacy stays "Data Not Collected".

DELETION IS ALWAYS CONFIRMED
Alike never deletes anything on its own. Every cleanup needs confirmation in
the app, iOS then presents its own, and the photos move to Recently Deleted
for about 30 days.

SUBSCRIPTION
Alike Pro is an auto-renewable subscription with yearly and monthly plans.
The free tier allows 3 scans per month, cleanup of one photo at a time,
guided review with Best Shot, one-tap enhancement, the Best Shot learning and
both widgets: the new 1.4.0 feature is not behind the paywall. Pro adds
unlimited scans, batch, screenshot and blurred cleanup, filters and
reminders.

The paywall is reachable from the Scanner tab, a group's Details view and
Settings. It states the plan length, automatic renewal unless cancelled at
least 24 hours before the period ends, payment charged to the Apple Account,
and the yearly plan's 7-day free trial. Restore Purchases and the Privacy
Policy and Terms of Use links are on the paywall and under Settings, then
Legal; the Terms name Apple's Standard EULA.

LANGUAGES AND DATA DELETION
The app, its legal and support pages and the permission alert are in twelve
languages; the listing in thirteen. Settings, then Data & Privacy, then
Delete Alike Data erases all scan results, cleanup history, Best Shot
learning and preferences on the device, and touches neither the photo
library, the permission, nor the subscription.
