# Legal and privacy parity

Review translation fidelity separately from legal validity. A faithful translation
can preserve a defect in English. Flag that defect for coordinated correction;
do not silently change the legal arrangement in one locale.

## Terms

Preserve each requirement wherever the English source contains it:

- Apple's Standard EULA governs the licence and prevails on conflicting licence terms.
- Yearly and monthly plans remain distinct. The 7-day trial is yearly-only and
  limited to eligible new subscribers.
- Cancellation requires at least 24 hours before the current period ends.
  Renewal charging occurs within the 24 hours before that end. Do not merge
  these separate statements or change their temporal direction.
- The developer cannot cancel subscriptions or issue refunds. Apple handles them.
- Governing law and court clauses retain mandatory local consumer protections,
  remedies, and access to local courts where the source preserves them.
- Apple remains a third-party beneficiary with enforcement rights. Preserve its
  support disclaimer, possible purchase-price refund, and other warranty limits.
- Preserve statutory exceptions to warranty, liability, termination, and refunds.
  Do not replace “intellectual property rights” with all rights or omit it.

## Privacy

Keep the actor and scope exact:

- Vision analysis runs on-device. Alike uploads no photos, thumbnails, feature
  prints, or analysis results.
- Preserve the exact exclusions for analytics, advertising, tracking, cookies,
  and the app's own network requests. Retain Apple-handled activity exceptions.
- Read the current crash-report section. If English permits local MetricKit
  reports that the user sends, do not turn “no third-party crash-reporting
  service” into “no crash reports”. Preserve consent, contents, and retention.
- Deletion needs the user's confirmation and the separate iOS confirmation.
  Photos enter the named Recently Deleted album for approximately 30 days.
- Notification permission is requested only for the weekly reminder. Do not
  turn this into a promise that the app never requests any permission.
- Delete Alike Data affects local Alike data. It does not delete photos, revoke
  photo permission, cancel a subscription, or revert an enhanced photo.

## Evidence and uncertainty

For a claim about current law or enforceability, consult current primary sources:
Apple's published EULA and policies, the applicable legislation, or regulator
publications. Cite the exact provision and jurisdiction. A language does not
uniquely determine the user's jurisdiction. Record unresolved applicability rather
than importing a generic US contract position into every locale.

Do not label a review “lawyer approved” or “legally compliant” without actual
qualified review. Keep proposed source corrections separate from translation edits.
When validation is authorized, reuse the repository's legal parity and site checks;
when prohibited, record them as pending. Do not invent a replacement check.
