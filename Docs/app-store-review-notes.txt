Thank you for reviewing Alike.

NO ACCOUNT IS NEEDED
Alike has no account, no sign-in and no server, so there is nothing to log
in to; the demo credential fields are intentionally empty. The app opens on
an introduction that hands off to the iOS photo permission alert.

PHOTO LIBRARY ACCESS
Alike compares library photos to find visually similar ones, so photo access
is the core permission. Please grant "Full Access"; with "Limited Access",
select three or four shots of one subject, or the scan reports no groups.

The permission is also used to write. Enhancing a best shot writes a
non-destructive PHContentEditingOutput adjustment: iOS keeps the original, no
copy is made, and it reverts from either app. It is never automatic: previewed
first, written only on Apply, not offered for video or in Limited Access.

Notification permission is requested only for the optional cleanup reminder.

WIDGETS, HOME SCREEN AND LOCK SCREEN
Two free widgets. One shows reclaimable space and review progress; the other
lists similar groups, screenshots and blurred photos. The first also comes in
three Lock Screen sizes, added from the Lock Screen's own Customize, and
StandBy shows them. They read a JSON snapshot in a shared App Group — counts,
byte totals and a date, never image data — and make no network requests. Taps
open `alike://` links; two library rows are Pro, so on a free account they
reach that category's paywall, which is intended.

HOW TO EXERCISE THE APP
1. Step through the introduction and grant photo access in the iOS alert.
2. Tap Scan on the Scanner tab. Analysis runs locally.
3. Open any group. A Best Shot is preselected; use Keep Best Only, Select
   All Except Best, or select by hand. For the enhancement: Enhance, hold to
   compare, Apply, then Revert to original.
4. Tap the delete action and confirm. iOS shows its own confirmation.

CRASH REPORTS, NEW IN 1.6.0
Alike keeps MetricKit crash payloads on the device. Only after a real crash,
the next calm launch shows one sheet, "Alike closed unexpectedly": Send
Report or Not Now. Send Report opens an addressed mail with the payload
attached (the share sheet without Mail); nothing goes unless the user sends
it. It asks once per crash, so a normal review will not see it.

ON-DEVICE, NOTHING COLLECTED
Analysis runs on device with Vision. No photo, feature print or result
leaves the device, and the only network requests are StoreKit's. Overriding a
best shot stores derived numbers in UserDefaults to tune the ranking. App
Privacy stays "Data Not Collected": all that can leave is a crash report the
user chooses to email from their own mail account.

DELETION IS ALWAYS CONFIRMED
Every cleanup is confirmed in the app and then by iOS, and photos move to
Recently Deleted for about 30 days.

SUBSCRIPTION
Alike Pro is an auto-renewable subscription with yearly and monthly plans.
The free tier allows 3 scans a month, one-photo-at-a-time cleanup, guided
review with Best Shot, enhancement, Best Shot learning and every widget. Pro
adds unlimited scans, batch, screenshot and blurred cleanup, filters and
reminders.

The paywall is reachable from the Scanner tab, a group's Details view and
Settings. It states the plan length, automatic renewal unless cancelled at
least 24 hours before the period ends, payment charged to the Apple Account,
and the yearly plan's 7-day free trial. Restore Purchases and the Privacy
Policy and Terms of Use links are on the paywall and under Settings → Legal;
the Terms name Apple's Standard EULA.

LANGUAGES AND DATA DELETION
The app, its legal and support pages and the permission alert are in twelve
languages; the listing, thirteen. Settings → Data & Privacy → Delete Alike
Data erases scan results, cleanup history, Best Shot learning, stored crash
reports and preferences, and touches neither the photo library, the
permission, nor the subscription.
