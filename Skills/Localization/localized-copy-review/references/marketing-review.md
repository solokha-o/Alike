# Marketing review

Apply these passes only to promotional copy. Do not apply persuasion techniques
to privacy disclosures, consumer-rights clauses, or billing conditions.

1. Check clarity: identify the concrete action, benefit, and next step.
2. Check voice: use the locale's register across metadata, headings, CTAs, and FAQ.
3. Check natural phrasing: remove English syntax and literal idioms that do not
   work in the target language. Preserve product names and actual control labels.
4. Check evidence: keep every feature, number, availability claim, and privacy
   promise tied to the source or current product evidence.
5. Check purchase expectations: preserve optional subscription status, plan
   duration, eligibility, trial conditions, and free allowance.
6. Re-read for claim drift after the stylistic edits.

Do not add testimonials, savings numbers, urgency, guarantees, or refund promises
without evidence and authorization. Do not make “always reversible” stronger than
the source's documented exception for replacing another app's edit.

If a protected subscription disclosure reads poorly, record the exact phrase and
its 3 owners. Improve adjacent unprotected copy without altering the disclosure.
A conversion-oriented external method must not override this rule.
