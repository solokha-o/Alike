# Language review

## Semantic pass

Compare complete paragraphs, not isolated words. For each material claim, identify:

| Element | Questions |
| --- | --- |
| Actor | Does Alike, iOS, Apple, or the user do the action? |
| Action and object | What is collected, stored, deleted, charged, or cancelled? |
| Scope | Does the claim cover photos, app data, permission, or subscriptions? |
| Condition | Does it require confirmation, eligibility, or a specific plan? |
| Strength | Is it a guarantee, possibility, obligation, prohibition, or exception? |
| Time and number | Are durations, deadlines, limits, and approximations unchanged? |
| Rights | Are exceptions and mandatory protections preserved? |

Classify defects as meaning, omission/addition, terminology, register, grammar,
or fluency. Treat changed billing, data-deletion scope, rights, or privacy promises
as substantive defects. Fix these before stylistic changes.

## Fluency pass

Read the target without consulting the English sentence structure. Replace literal
phrases, unclear pronouns, unnatural collocations, and mixed registers. Preserve
legal qualifiers even when they make the sentence longer. Do not rewrite correct
text merely to demonstrate activity.

Check articles, grammatical gender, case, agreement, and punctuation. After a noun
replacement, review the whole phrase. For Turkish, replacing `kitaplık` with `arşiv`
also changes suffix vowels: `kitaplığındaki` becomes `arşivindeki`, not
`arşivındaki`. Do not use substring replacement without reviewing every result.

For Traditional Chinese, check vocabulary and script, not only character conversion.
For regional variants, preserve the requested regional vocabulary.

## Final pass

Re-read the target and source after all edits. A back-translation can expose a
missing condition, but it is not independent evidence of natural phrasing.
If another reviewer is authorized, provide the source and target without your
preferred edits. Do not count a second pass by the same model as human confirmation.

Report exact unresolved phrases, their files, and why the source does not resolve
those phrases. Avoid numeric quality scores without a defined evaluation method.
