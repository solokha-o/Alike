Thank you for reviewing Alike.

NO ACCOUNT IS NEEDED
Alike has no account, no sign-in and no server, so there is nothing to log in
to. The demo credential fields are intentionally empty and a demo account is
not required. The app opens into a short introduction; its last page explains
why photo access is needed and ends with a neutral "Continue" button that hands
off to the iOS permission alert, which asks in its own words.

PHOTO LIBRARY ACCESS
Alike compares the photos in the library to find visually similar ones, so
photo library access is the app's core permission. Please grant "Full Access"
when prompted. If you prefer "Limited Access", select several visually similar
photos (for example three or four shots of the same subject taken in a row),
otherwise the scan will correctly report that it found no groups.

Notification permission is requested only if you turn on the cleanup reminder
in Settings; it is not needed to review the app.

HOW TO EXERCISE THE APP
1. Step through the introduction and tap "Continue" on its last page, then
   grant photo library access in the iOS alert.
2. Tap Scan on the Scanner tab. Analysis runs locally and progress is shown.
3. Open any group. A Best Shot is preselected; use Keep Best Only, Select All
   Except Best, or select photos by hand.
4. Tap the delete action and confirm. iOS then shows its own confirmation.

ON-DEVICE ANALYSIS
All similarity analysis runs on device with the Apple Vision framework. No
photo, thumbnail, feature print or analysis result leaves the device, and the
app makes no network requests of its own. The only outbound activity is
handled by StoreKit when a subscription is purchased or restored.

DELETION IS ALWAYS CONFIRMED
Alike never deletes anything on its own. Every cleanup requires explicit
confirmation in the app, iOS presents its own system confirmation, and the
photos are moved to the iOS Recently Deleted album, where they remain
recoverable for about 30 days. The app never erases photos permanently.

SUBSCRIPTION
Alike Pro is an auto-renewable subscription with yearly and monthly plans.
The free tier allows 3 scans per month and cleanup of one photo at a time.
Alike Pro adds unlimited scans, batch cleanup, screenshot cleanup, blurred
photo cleanup, advanced filters and custom cleanup reminders.

The paywall is reachable from three places: the Scanner tab, the Details view
of a group, and Settings. It states the subscription length, that renewal is
automatic unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current
period, that payment is charged to the Apple Account, and that the yearly plan
includes a 7-day free trial for eligible new subscribers with billing starting
when the trial ends. Restore Purchases is available on the paywall and in
Settings. Links to the Privacy Policy and to the Terms of Use are shown on the
paywall and under Settings, then Legal. The Terms of Use page names Apple's
Standard EULA as the licence that governs use of the app and defers to it on
any conflict.

LANGUAGES
The app, the App Store listing and the Privacy Policy, Terms of Use and
Support pages are localized into twelve languages: English, Ukrainian, German,
French, Spanish, Latin American Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese, Italian, Dutch,
Polish, Turkish and Traditional Chinese. The iOS photo library permission
alert itself stays in English; its text is a build setting rather than a
localized string, and that is a known gap.

DATA DELETION
Settings, then Data & Privacy, then Delete Alike Data erases all scan results,
cleanup history and preferences stored on the device. It does not touch the
photo library, the photo permission or the subscription.
