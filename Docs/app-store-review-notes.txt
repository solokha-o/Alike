Thank you for reviewing Alike.

NO ACCOUNT IS NEEDED
Alike has no account, no sign-in and no server, so there is nothing to log
in to; the demo credential fields are intentionally empty. The app opens
into a short introduction that hands off to the iOS photo permission alert.

PHOTO LIBRARY ACCESS
Alike compares library photos to find visually similar ones, so photo access
is the core permission. Please grant "Full Access". With "Limited Access",
select several similar photos (three or four shots of one subject in a row),
or the scan correctly reports no groups.

The same permission is now also used to write. Enhancing a best shot writes
a non-destructive adjustment through PHContentEditingOutput, stamped
com.alike.autoEnhance: iOS keeps the original, no copy is created, and the
edit appears in Apple Photos like any other and can be reverted from either
app — in Alike with Revert to original, in Photos with Edit then Revert. It
is never automatic: it is offered as a preview and written only after you
tap Apply. It is not offered for video, in Limited Access, or for assets
marked non-editable, and a photo that already carries another app's edit is
replaced only after you agree.

Notification permission is requested only for the optional cleanup reminder.

HOW TO EXERCISE THE APP
1. Step through the introduction and grant photo access in the iOS alert.
2. Tap Scan on the Scanner tab. Analysis runs locally.
3. Open any group. A Best Shot is preselected; use Keep Best Only, Select
   All Except Best, or select by hand. For the enhancement, tap Enhance on
   the Best Shot, hold the preview to compare it with the original, tap
   Apply, then tap Revert to original.
4. Tap the delete action and confirm. iOS then shows its own confirmation.

ON-DEVICE ANALYSIS
All similarity analysis runs on device with the Apple Vision framework. No
photo, thumbnail, feature print or analysis result leaves the device, and
the app makes no network requests except StoreKit's, for purchases and
restores.

BEST SHOT LEARNS FROM YOUR OWN PICKS
When you override the suggested best shot, Alike stores the difference
between the two photos' quality signals — derived numbers, not the photos —
and uses it to nudge the ranking towards what you pick. It learns only from
your own actions in the app; nothing is collected or sent, and the values
live in UserDefaults on device. Settings resets the learning, and Delete
Alike Data erases it too. App Privacy stays "Data Not Collected".

DELETION IS ALWAYS CONFIRMED
Alike never deletes anything on its own. Every cleanup needs explicit
confirmation in the app, iOS then presents its own, and the photos move to
Recently Deleted, recoverable for about 30 days; nothing is erased
permanently.

SUBSCRIPTION
Alike Pro is an auto-renewable subscription with yearly and monthly plans.
The free tier allows 3 scans per month, cleanup of one photo at a time,
guided review with Best Shot, one-tap enhancement and the Best Shot
learning: neither new 1.3.0 feature is behind the paywall. Pro adds
unlimited scans, batch, screenshot and blurred cleanup, advanced filters and
custom reminders.

The paywall is reachable from the Scanner tab, a group's Details view and
Settings. It states the plan length, automatic renewal unless cancelled at
least 24 hours before the current period ends, payment charged to the Apple
Account, and the yearly plan's 7-day free trial for eligible new
subscribers. Restore Purchases and links to the Privacy Policy and Terms of
Use are on the paywall and under Settings, then Legal. The Terms of Use name
Apple's Standard EULA as the governing licence.

LANGUAGES
The app, its legal and support pages and the permission alert are localized
into twelve languages, the listing into thirteen.

DATA DELETION
Settings, then Data & Privacy, then Delete Alike Data erases all scan
results, cleanup history, Best Shot learning and preferences on the device.
It does not touch the photo library, the permission or the subscription.
