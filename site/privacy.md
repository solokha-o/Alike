Last updated: August 5, 2026

Alike is an iOS app that finds and groups visually similar photos in your photo library so you can review them and free up space. Alike is operated by Oleksandr Solokha, an individual developer based in Ukraine.

This Privacy Policy explains what Alike does and does not collect, where your data lives, and what control you have over it.

**The short version: Alike has no account, no sign-in, and no server. Your photos and everything Alike learns about them stay on your device.**

## What Alike Accesses

### Your photo library

Alike asks for photo library access once, at the start. This access is required for the app's only purpose: comparing photos to find visually similar ones.

You can grant full access, or grant limited access and choose which photos Alike may see. You can change or revoke this at any time in iOS Settings, under Privacy & Security → Photos → Alike.

Alike reads photo image data and the metadata iOS provides for each asset, such as creation date, approximate capture location when present, file size, and whether the photo is a screenshot or a favourite. This information is used to pre-filter which photos are worth comparing and to show you group details.

### Notifications

Alike requests notification permission only if you turn on the weekly cleanup reminder in Settings, and never otherwise. If you never enable that reminder, Alike never asks.

If you enable it, the reminder is scheduled locally on your device by iOS. Alike does not use push notifications and does not register for or receive any remote notification tokens.

## What Alike Does With Your Photos

### Analysis runs entirely on your device

Alike compares photos using Apple's Vision framework, which runs locally on your iPhone. Each photo is reduced to a numeric feature print, and photos are grouped when those feature prints are close enough under the sensitivity level you choose.

**Your photos are never uploaded.** No photo, thumbnail, feature print, or analysis result is transmitted off your device by Alike. There is no Alike server to send them to.

### Results are stored locally

Alike caches its results on your device using Core Data and local files, so you do not have to rescan every time you open the app. This local storage includes photo identifiers, feature prints, group membership, estimated sizes, your review progress and selections, cleanup history, and your app preferences.

All of it lives in Alike's own sandboxed storage on your device and is included in your device backups if you back up your device.

## What Alike Does Not Collect

Alike contains no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind. Specifically, Alike does not:

- upload your photos or any data derived from them;
- collect analytics or usage events;
- use advertising identifiers (IDFA) or ask for tracking permission;
- build a profile about you;
- share, sell, or rent any personal information, because it does not collect any to share;
- use cookies or web beacons.

Alike makes no network requests of its own. The only outbound activity is handled by Apple: subscription purchases through StoreKit, and opening App Store or email links when you tap them.

## Deleting Photos

Deletion is always your decision and always requires explicit confirmation.

When you confirm a cleanup, Alike asks iOS to delete the photos you selected. iOS shows its own system confirmation before anything is removed. Deleted photos go to your **Recently Deleted** album, where iOS keeps them for about 30 days and you can restore them.

Alike never deletes photos silently, never deletes photos you did not select, and cannot bypass the iOS confirmation.

## Subscriptions and Purchases

Alike Pro is an auto-renewable subscription sold through Apple. Purchases are processed entirely by Apple using StoreKit.

Alike receives from Apple only what it needs to unlock paid features and restore purchases: your entitlement status, the product identifier of your plan, and the transaction state. **Alike never receives your payment card details, billing address, or Apple Account credentials.**

Apple's handling of your purchase information is governed by [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). Subscriptions, cancellations, and refunds are managed through Apple and the App Store.

## Your Controls

| What you want | How to do it |
| --- | --- |
| Change or limit photo access | iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos → Alike |
| Stop notifications | Turn off the weekly cleanup reminder in Alike Settings, or use iOS Settings → Notifications |
| Erase everything Alike stores | Alike Settings → Data & Privacy → Delete Alike Data |
| Remove all data completely | Delete the Alike app from your device |

### Delete Alike Data

Settings → Data & Privacy → **Delete Alike Data** erases everything Alike has stored on your device: scan results and analysis caches, cleanup progress and history, and your Alike preferences. This action cannot be undone.

It does **not** touch your photos, your Recently Deleted album, your photo access permission, or your Alike Pro subscription. After deletion, Alike shows its introduction again without asking for photo access a second time.

Deleting the app removes all of Alike's local data along with it.

## Legal Basis and Your Rights

Because Alike does not collect or transmit personal data, there is no personal data held by the developer to access, correct, export, or erase, and no data processed on any legal basis such as consent or legitimate interest.

All processing happens locally on your device under your control, and you can erase it yourself at any time using Delete Alike Data or by deleting the app.

If you are in the EU, the UK, Ukraine, California, or another region with data protection rights, those rights still apply — there is simply no server-side data set for them to operate on. If you believe otherwise, contact us at the address above and we will respond.

## Children

Alike is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them. The app has no account system, no user-generated content, and no communication features.
